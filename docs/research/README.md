# Research notes

These are the literature reviews behind the README section "What does the research say?" and the in-app research text. They are research notes, not part of the app. Every DOI in them was checked against Crossref or DataCite, and each source is marked as read in full, as abstract only, or as grey literature.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [dyslexia-reading-support.md](dyslexia-reading-support.md) | The main review. It checks each claim in the README against later evidence and adds newer meta-analyses, a Cochrane review and randomised trials (4 November 2026). |
| [danish-nordic-research.md](danish-nordic-research.md) | Danish and Nordic research: why Danish is hard to learn to read, whether Denmark is behind, and what carries over to Danish from other languages. |
| [international-research.md](international-research.md) | Research from German-, French-, Dutch-, Italian-, Spanish-, Hebrew-, Arabic- and Chinese-speaking countries; international evidence bodies (WWC, EEF, Campbell); how added cues help and when they fade. |
| [amass-trials-and-literature.md](amass-trials-and-literature.md) | Registered trials (ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP) and literature filtered by journal quality. |

Statements marked "design inference" are suggestions for the app. They are not findings of the studies.
